Before you write in

The five questions that come up most often, answered ahead of the first call.

  • Do you work with clients outside the UAE?

    Yes. Writing and editing work is handled remotely for clients across the Gulf, Europe and the United States. Hosting and moderating is Dubai based by default, and travel can be arranged for the right brief.

  • Do you host events in Arabic as well as English?

    Yes. I present and moderate in both languages, and regularly run bilingual events where the room switches between the two.

  • How do you price a project?

    Most work is quoted as a fixed project fee once the scope is clear. Day rates apply to hosting and on site work. A rate card is available on request.

  • Can you write under our own byline?

    Yes. Ghost writing for executives is a regular part of the work, covering opinion pieces, keynote scripts, forewords and LinkedIn commentary.

  • What do you need from us to start?

    The audience, the outcome you want, any existing brand or style guidance, and the deadline. If those are not settled yet, the first call is usually spent working them out.
